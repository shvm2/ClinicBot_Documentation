Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

ClinicBot ("ClinicBot", "we", "us", or "our") is a WhatsApp-based chatbot designed to assist users with clinic-related communication and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClinicBot collects, how that information is used, and how it is stored.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with ClinicBot through WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number associated with the conversation.
  • Messages and information that you voluntarily send to ClinicBot.
  • Information contained within those messages that is necessary to provide the requested service.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the functionality of the clinic service.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by ClinicBot may be used to:

  • Respond to your messages and requests.
  • Provide clinic-related information and services.
  • Maintain and manage your conversations with the bot.
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of ClinicBot.
  • Maintain records necessary for providing the service.
  • Address technical issues, security concerns, or misuse of the service.

3. Storage of Information

ClinicBot stores WhatsApp phone numbers and messages sent to the bot.

We retain this information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain the service, unless a longer retention period is required by law or otherwise communicated to you.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information may be accessed by authorized individuals or service providers involved in operating, maintaining, or securing ClinicBot, where necessary to provide the service.

We may also disclose information when required to do so by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

5. WhatsApp

ClinicBot operates through WhatsApp. Your interaction with ClinicBot is also subject to WhatsApp's applicable terms and privacy practices.

ClinicBot does not control how WhatsApp processes information independently of ClinicBot.

6. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may stop interacting with ClinicBot at any time.

If you would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your ClinicBot conversations, you may contact the ClinicBot administrator using the contact information provided below.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a data request.

7. Children's Privacy

ClinicBot is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to ClinicBot without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete the information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to ClinicBot, our data practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published at the same Privacy Policy URL and will include a revised "Last updated" date.
